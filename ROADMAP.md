# LingMate Roadmap

## Vision
LingMate is an AI language-learning companion for adults who want to **actually use** a language: people who have moved abroad, are planning a move, travel, work with the language, or simply love it. Practice is built around real-life tasks from the learner's own life, grounded in SLA research, and designed to become a daily habit. Yuji (the mascot, `static/yuji-lingmate.png`) is the face of the app and stays.

**Reference user (dogfooding):** Roman — Russian L1, English C1, Italian A1, Spanish A2 → B1/B2, living in Vitoria-Gasteiz (Spain), LAMS master's student. Spanish (es-ES) for a relocated learner is the first fully polished path. Design every data model and scenario so other languages, places and goals plug in later without rewrites (profile-driven, template-based), but don't spend runs building content for other user types before Roman's path works end to end.

**North star (Roman, 2026-11-09):** LingMate should replace a human tutor almost completely: real learning along a **curriculum** (not just chatting), in a **friendly peer atmosphere** (Yuji is a study buddy, not a strict teacher), and **not limited by API quotas**. A tutor diagnoses, plans, explains, practises, corrects, remembers, motivates and assesses — every one of these must exist in the app. Thesis background: Roman's VKR (ITMO 2026) already names the "pedagogical core" (CEFR curriculum + spaced repetition), PCIC-based content and fine-tuning small models for RU→ES errors as next steps.
**Quota strategy:** (1) curriculum content (explanations, examples, exercises, dialogues, audio scripts) is generated once, reviewed and stored — not regenerated per learner; (2) controlled practice is checked without an LLM; (3) the LLM is used only for open dialogue and feedback, with short prompts; (4) a router spreads calls over free tiers (5.8) and local models (5.7) with fallback; (5) any paid option only with Roman's decision.

Pedagogy: `docs/research/` (morphosyntax.md, sla.md, syllabus-design.md, multilingual-education.md, phonetics.md, research-methods.md, engagement.md). Cite sections in PRs.

Content pre-generated and stored (quota strategy). (morphosyntax §2–6, syllabus-design §3–6, sla §4)
